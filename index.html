16 December 2014 Eternal Memorial

APS Peshawar Martyrs — Forever In Our Hearts

Paying solemn national tribute to the 144+ innocent martyrs of Army Public School Peshawar. Their sacred sacrifice united Pakistan forever in courage, resilience, and patriotism.

144+ Innocent Martyrs & Teachers
"Unke guman me tha ke hami mar jayenge, Hum wo chirag hain jo hawao se jalenge!"

On 16 December 2014, the brave children and heroic teachers of Army Public School Peshawar stood fearless. Their extraordinary courage ignited an unshakeable national unity. Today, 240+ Million Pakistanis salute their eternal legacy.

🤲 "May Allah Almighty grant highest ranks in Jannah to our APS Peshawar Angels."
